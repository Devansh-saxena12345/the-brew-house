Our Story

A Little House of Big Brews

The Brewhouse began in 2018 as a two-person dream: a tiny counter, one espresso machine, and an obsession with the perfect cup. Word spread, the queue grew, and so did the kitchen — but the heart stayed the same.

Today we roast our beans in small batches, bake fresh every morning, and treat every guest like a regular. Whether you're here for your first coffee of the day or your last dessert of the night, our door (and our kettle) is always on.
